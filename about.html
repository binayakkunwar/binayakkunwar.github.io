Academic Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Agricultural Economics at Purdue University. My research focuses on farmland markets, agricultural finance, land-use change, renewable energy development, and applied econometrics.

My dissertation examines how regional development, energy policy, information, and market structure shape farmland prices and landowner behavior, with emphasis on spatial markets, causal identification, and policy-relevant land market outcomes.
