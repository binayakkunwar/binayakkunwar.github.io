About

Research at the intersection of land, finance, and policy.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Agricultural Economics at Purdue University. My work centers on farmland markets and agricultural finance, with related research in land-use economics, renewable energy, commodity marketing, production economics, and applied econometrics.

I earned an M.S. in Agricultural Economics from Purdue University in 2024 and a B.S. in Agriculture from Agriculture and Forestry University in Nepal in 2021.

Research interests: Agricultural Finance · Land Use Economics · Agribusiness · Farm Management · Commodity Marketing · Agricultural & Food Policy · Production Economics

Selected working papers

Current research

Working Paper

Identification of Margin-Specific IV Elasticities in Unordered Outcome Models: Evidence from Farmland Conversion to Renewable Energy

with Chad Fiechter and Guy Tchuente

Develops an identification framework for margin-specific IV elasticities when outcomes are unordered, with an application to farmland conversion to renewable energy.

Manuscript ↗
Dissertation Chapter

The Economic Development Premium in Farmland Markets

with Chad Fiechter, Guy Tchuente, and Aaron Shew

Studies how a concentrated state-led land acquisition and regional development shock is capitalized into nearby farmland prices and spatial spillovers.

Current manuscript link will be added when the latest version is posted.

Dissertation Chapter

When Solar Projects Fail: Causal Evidence on Farmland Prices

with Chad Fiechter and Aaron Shew

Examines how failed renewable-energy projects and reversals in development expectations affect farmland prices.

Dissertation Chapter

Market Structure, Information, and Market Power in Farmland Transactions

with Chad Fiechter and Timothy Cason

Uses laboratory markets to study how market thinness and information shape bargaining, prices, and market power in farmland transactions.

Working Paper

Farmer Corn Marketing and Over-the-Counter Derivatives

with Chad Fiechter, Mindy Mallory, and Eugene Faller

Studies U.S. corn producers' use of structured over-the-counter risk-management products and the market conditions associated with product choice.
